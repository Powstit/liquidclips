# UAT Task Cards · Session Script

**Purpose:** Give the moderator ONE card per task. Read the task verbatim. Do NOT paraphrase or hint. Wait for the participant to act. Silence is your friend.

---

## Card 1 · Sign in + reach Home
> "Please open the app and get signed in. Land on the main dashboard."

**Timer:** starts when you say "go" · stops when `.lc-app` is visible on Home.

**Success:** ≤ 90 seconds elapsed AND user did not ask for help AND user is on `#/home`.

**Observations to log:**
- Did they understand the Whop connection required?
- Did the founder video interrupt / help / annoy?
- Did they read the nav or just click?
- If they asked "how do I sign in" — that's a P0.

---

## Card 2 · Make your first clip
> "Use this YouTube URL [paste 10-min video URL] and create one clip you'd actually post to TikTok."

**Timer:** starts when you say "go" · stops when they say "I'd post that" OR they abandon.

**Success:** ≤ 8 minutes elapsed AND rendered .mp4 with watermark AND user is not visibly frustrated.

**Observations to log:**
- Where did they paste the URL? (command bar vs source panel)
- Did they discover Kade? (auto-clip)
- Did they trim / add captions? Manual or via command?
- Did they notice when clips arrived?
- Did they choose the right clip from the strip?
- Did they export?

---

## Card 3 · Find a bounty
> "You want to earn money by clipping. Find a paid opportunity in the app and start working on it."

**Timer:** starts when you say "go" · stops when they reach Whop checkout or bounty modal.

**Success:** ≤ 3 minutes elapsed AND they landed on a real bounty.

**Observations to log:**
- Did they go to Earn tab?
- Or Campaigns?
- Or did they get lost?
- Did they understand the $ flow?
- If they can't find bounties in 3 min — that's a P1.

---

## Card 4 · Recovery
> "Pretend your export just failed. Something went wrong. Show me what you'd do next."

**Timer:** starts when you say "go" · stops when they reach Copy Diagnostics OR support OR they give up.

**Success:** ≤ 60 seconds elapsed AND they reached SOME support surface.

**Observations to log:**
- Did they know Copy Diagnostics existed?
- Did they look for a Help button?
- Did they try to email / Telegram?
- Did they give up? (P0 if yes)

---

## Card 5 · Free exploration (5 min)
> "You've got 5 minutes to poke around wherever you want. Show me anything that confuses you or feels missing."

**Success criteria:** They surface at least 2 concrete issues you can log.

**Observations to log:**
- What tabs / surfaces did they visit unprompted?
- What did they SAY they'd want that doesn't exist?
- Any moments of "wait, what?"
- Any moments of "oh that's cool"?
