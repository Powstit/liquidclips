### 2. Canonical owner change

**Before:** No boot topic proved which bundle actually rendered. `boot` from `bootDiag()` fired at the diagnostic layer but did not carry `source_sha` or `bundle_index_html_sha256` — the two fields required to distinguish "stale bundle rendered" from "bundle up-to-date but telemetry buffer never flushed." Route-change telemetry emitted four per-phase topics (`nav_click_performance` per-phase / `route_mount_performance` / `first_contentful_render_performance` / `interactive_ready_performance`) but no single event carrying the full waterfall, forcing HQ to join four topics per click to reconstruct a click's timeline.

**After:** `desktop-2/src/lib/navPerf.ts::emitBootTelemetry()` is the canonical owner of the "boot proof" contract. It reads meta tags (`runtime-version`, `source-sha`) with a shell fallback, then async-computes `bundle_index_html_sha256` via WebCrypto and emits the `boot` topic exactly once per session. `markInteractiveReady()` additionally emits a consolidated `nav_click_performance { route, click_ts, mount_ts, content_ready_ts }` topic at the end of every route change — one event per click, joined-timeline shape.

**Duplicate writers removed:** none — `bootDiag()` continues to emit its own `boot` payload (env/mode/dev/prod/sentry/etc). The new `emitBootTelemetry()` is a SIBLING emit under the SAME topic name because the two payloads answer different questions: `bootDiag()` answers "what environment am I in?" and `emitBootTelemetry()` answers "which bundle rendered?" Both consumers (HQ Doctor + Money Funnel) already tolerate multi-emit under a single topic; the `data` payload disambiguates. If a future refactor wants to merge the two, the merge is safe because both are idempotent single-emits and neither reads the other's state.

> **Wave contract rule:** the two `boot` emissions serve two distinct consumers and were intentionally left as separate emits. `bootDiag`'s payload has been in the graph since Phase 1 recovery brief; renaming it would break Doctor Lite's env-mode dashboard.
