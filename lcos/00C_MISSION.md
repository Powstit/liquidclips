# 00C · Mission

> **Help creators earn recurring income from clipping.**

Everything LCOS analyzes must eventually climb back to this promise. Every bug must answer: *"What customer promise is broken?"*

## The four fingerprints of the mission

Every capability, feature, journey, and station in LCOS is scored against how it moves at least one of these:

| # | Fingerprint | Question |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Reach** | Can a creator find + start clipping without friction? |
| M2 | **Revenue** | Does the creator's activity become recurring MRR? |
| M3 | **Trust** | Do we tell the truth about money, tier, and progress? |
| M4 | **Retention** | Does the creator come back after their first success? |

If a feature does not measurably serve M1–M4, it is a candidate for removal.

## What the mission excludes

Explicitly not our mission (kept here so scope stays honest):

- We are not a general video editor.
- We are not a social network.
- We are not a payments processor.
- We are not a marketing suite.

Whop is the payment rail. Ayrshare + Postiz are the distribution rail. Backend is the audit rail. The mission is the money the creator keeps.

## Mission gates

Every LCOS phase must produce output that traces to at least one fingerprint. If a proof cannot cite a mission fingerprint, the proof is malformed.
